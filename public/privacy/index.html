Privacy

How openvaultdb.com uses Google Analytics and Google-hosted resources, and the one choice you control: analytics. For accounts, see “This website” below.

Effective 19 October 2026

This website

openvaultdb.com is a static documentation site. It has no advertising and does not sell data. The optional OpenVaultDB account (sign-in through Firebase Authentication, a Google service) is a connection directory of pointers to vaults you own; it never holds your vault data.

Fonts and sign-in scripts from Google

To display the site, your browser loads fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com) on every page. The home page, Docs, Authentication model, Connect and the My vaults and My apps pages also load the Firebase SDK and sign-in components when the page opens: scripts from www.gstatic.com and apis.google.com, a sign-in frame from openvaultdb.firebaseapp.com, and requests to www.googleapis.com. For these loads Google receives your IP address and browser details. They set no analytics cookies and do not depend on your analytics choice.

Analytics

Google Analytics, provided by Google Ireland Limited, runs on openvaultdb.com only if you choose “Accept analytics” in the analytics banner. Until you accept, no analytics script loads and no analytics cookie is set.

After you accept, Google Analytics sets first-party cookies (_ga and _ga_<id>, kept for up to two years) and receives the page address without its query or fragment, browser and device information, and an approximate location that Google derives from your IP address. Google Signals and advertising features are switched off.

The legal basis is your consent. We keep Google Analytics event data for no longer than 14 months.

Your choice and withdrawing it

Your choice is stored in your browser (localStorage, key openvaultdb.analytics-consent.v1). You can change it at any time with “Analytics choices” in the site footer or the button below. Choosing “Reject” stops analytics and deletes the _ga cookies on this site.
